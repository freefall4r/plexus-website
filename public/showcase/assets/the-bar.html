The bar, standing in your own room.

A Victorian pub bar in walnut — panelled, fluted, brass-railed — built into the terrace you already have, under the timber roof you already have. Everything below is measured, not sketched.

Walk around it

The picture above is a lighting study. This is the model itself — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and every number on this page is measured off it.

The buttons along the bottom put you in a particular spot — behind the bar, the booth, the way in. Roof puts the timber roof on and takes it off. Booth length is live: drag it and the banquette grows, the tables follow, and the model checks it still fits as you go.

Three places to stand

Every dimension here is the one in the model.

From your side of the counter

The counter runs 2400 along the clad wall, 1100 high, with a 260 overhang so five people can actually get their knees under it. Frame-and-panel face, fluted pilasters, a moulded top edge and a brass foot rail that stops dead at the mirror rather than crossing it.

From behind it

A 700 service aisle — enough to turn round in with the door open. The bottles sit on the gantry behind you, lit from under each shelf. Nothing stands on the worktop: 530 of it stays clear at bar height, which is the difference between a bar you can work and a bar you can only look at.

The banquette

Three metres of deep-buttoned oxblood hide, diamond-tufted on the back and channel-fluted on the wings — two different tufts, which is what stops a banquette looking fake. It dies into the chimney breast and opens into the room, with two dark stone tops on disc-footed pedestals.

The two tape readings

The room is modelled at your tape (west wall 5400) rather than the drawing (6040). Two one-minute measurements settle it for good: the existing bar across its front, and the pool table across its frame.

Cue room

The table has 930 clear on its tightest sides. A full 1450 cue needs a room 4460 deep and this one is 4240 — 220 short even empty — so a short rest on the long sides comes with the room, not with the layout.
