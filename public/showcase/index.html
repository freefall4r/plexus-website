The idea

الفكرة

NAWAH is a seed you sit inside. A sculptural pod that wraps around the reader — back, sides and overhead — leaving one open mouth of daylight and a cushion of olive-green wool.

The whole shell is cut from 18 mm birch plywood on our CNC, layer by layer, then threaded together on steel rods. No carving, no moulds — the drama comes from repetition: dozens of precise flat parts becoming one continuous curve.

It ships flat, assembles with a wrench, and every plywood edge is left exposed as the finish — the stripes of the laminations are the ornament.

Six ways to build it

ستة خيارات

One idea, explored properly. Three silhouettes — the balanced original, a slimmer taller cocoon, and a wider fuller pod — each buildable in two constructions: nested rings that wrap front-to-back, or vertical ribs that stand side by side. Three of the six carry the brass reading light.

Engineered before it exists
